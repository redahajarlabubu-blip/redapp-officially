    The desktop versions open Redapp in its own window. They are the web app packaged for desktop, not a native installer.

    How to install an APK
    1. Download the file to your phone.
    2. Open it. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager.
    3. Tap Install, then open Redapp.
    How to run the desktop version
    1. Extract the ZIP to a folder.
    2. Windows: double-click Run-Redapp-Windows.bat. Linux: run sh run-redapp-linux.sh in a terminal.
    3. Redapp opens in its own window with Chrome, Edge, Chromium or Brave, or in your default browser.

    Questions, answered.

    How do I start a chat?

    Tap Sender to show your QR code and ask the other person to tap Receiver and scan it. Or tap New chat and enter their phone number.

    Do I need a phone number?

    Only for chats by number. Add your own number first so people can reply to you. QR chats do not need one.

    What can I send?

    Text, voice messages, stickers, photos, files, links and your location.

    What if I lose my connection?

    Redapp keeps your messages and sends them when you are back online.

    Which devices does it run on?

    Redapp is available as an Android APK, and as desktop versions for Windows and Linux that open Redapp in their own window.

    The install is blocked. What now?

    Open your Android settings and allow installs from the app you used to download the file, then open the APK again.

    What can Redapp AI do?

    It can chat, tell the time and date, do math, convert units and tell jokes. You can even teach it new answers. It runs on your device, so it works offline.

    How do I turn on notifications?

    Choose Turn on notifications in Redapp. If they are blocked, allow notifications for Redapp in your phone or browser settings.

    Redapp will not connect. What can I try?

    In Settings, test the relay connection. Some networks block relays, so try another network, such as mobile data, and test again.

    How do I run the Windows or Linux version?

    Extract the ZIP. On Windows, double-click Run-Redapp-Windows.bat. On Linux, run sh run-redapp-linux.sh in a terminal. You need Chrome, Edge, Chromium or Brave for the app window, otherwise it opens in your browser.
